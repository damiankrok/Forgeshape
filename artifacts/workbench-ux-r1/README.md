# Workbench look and function (WORKBENCH-UX-R1)

The record of the owner's request to audit the look and function of the tablet
workbench and the Windows desktop against SolidCraft and Shapr3D, research how
CAD applications look and work, ask a design council, and iterate five times.

- `RESEARCH.md` — the online research digest: 74 sourced patterns and the
  platform numbers (Fusion, Onshape, SolidWorks, SolidCraft, Shapr3D,
  Plasticity, Material 3, Fluent 2, WCAG 2.2).
- `COUNCIL_PLAN.md` — the chair's synthesis of two audits (visual craft;
  interaction and function) and five council seats (CAD power user, touch and
  pen designer, visual designer, accessibility and ergonomics, product lead):
  the resolved conflicts, five ordered iterations and what was deferred.

What each iteration actually delivered, and its evidence, is recorded in
`PROJECT_STATUS.md`; the council plan is a plan, not a statement of what is
implemented.
