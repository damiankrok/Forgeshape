## 3. The capture finding (A2), measured and fixed

**Symptom.** In the first `after/` frames, taken on `e191dad` (run
`36333563367`), frame `10_cut_preview` showed an outward prism in the New Body
tint and no Cut tint. At that moment native held Cut and AgainstNormal, the
candidate was valid, and the commit made the correct pocket. Frame
`02b_disk_selected` still showed the ring after the disk had been chosen, while
the Android HUD in the same frame had already moved to the disk.

**Cause, read from the renderer's own log** (`logcat-final.txt` of that run):

| Time | Renderer event |
| --- | --- |
| 16:40:19.716 | frame 8: the New Body preview prism uploaded |
| 16:40:19.984 | the operation set to Cut |
| 16:40:20.592 | frame 10: **the Cut result uploaded into the target body** (`src=16:96`, body 5) |
| 16:40:20.965 | frame 11: the dragged Cut uploaded |
| 16:40:21.286 | frame 12 |
| 16:40:22.059 | the screenshot logged (taken about 0.4 s earlier, then PNG-encoded) |

- **The Cut preview was drawn.** The renderer drew and presented it as the
  product specifies.
- **The screenshot was early.** On this emulator the renderer presents about
  three frames a second at 1080 × 2400, through a four-image FIFO swapchain. A
  capture 400 ms after a change therefore showed a frame recorded before the
  change.
- **Not a product defect.** On a GPU a frame is milliseconds. The device test's
  assertions all read native state, and none of them depended on the pixels.

**Fix, in the test only (`db8ff6b`).**

- `NativeViewport.debugRendererFramesPresented` mirrors the renderer's
  presented-frame count out of the render thread, the way
  `debugRendererDeviceRebuilds` already did. It is a count and nothing else.
- `CadVerticalSliceTest` waits for six presented frames before every
  screenshot, and records how many it saw and how long that took.
- **On the candidate:** every one of the 18 captures saw 6 frames, in
  1.49–2.12 s. That is the emulator's real latency, and more than four times
  the old fixed delay.
- `after/10_cut_preview` now shows the body tinted Cut, with the through-pocket
  and the arrow into the body. `after/02b` shows the disk's cylinder.
