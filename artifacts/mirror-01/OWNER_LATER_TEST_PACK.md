What no emulator settles. None of this is claimed as verified, and no verdict is

## MIRROR-01 — Construction Mirror across world XY / XZ / YZ

1. **Plane-choice clarity.** The chooser offers `XY`, `XZ` and `YZ`, the same
   three names the sketch workplanes already carry, and each names the axis it
   reflects in its content description. At a real desk, with a real object on
   screen, does a user pick the plane they meant on the first try — or do they
   have to try one and Undo? That is the question this stage cannot answer.
2. **Mirror discoverability in Objects.** Mirror is the fifth entry in the row
   overflow's strip, on its second line beside Duplicate. Is it findable at all
   without being told it is there, and does living behind `⋯` put it too far
   from a common act?
3. **The two-line strip.** The strip wraps because five 48 dp targets do not fit
   192 dp. Does it read as one strip on two lines, or as two unrelated rows?
4. **Visual correctness on rotated and non-uniformly scaled bodies.** The
   arithmetic is proved exact by `MIRROR01-04..08` over six primitives, three
   planes and ten poses. What is not proved is whether a reflected, turned,
   stretched body *looks* right next to its original through a real camera at a
   real angle.
5. **All three planes.** The device journey drives `YZ` only. `XY` and `XZ`
   through the real controls, on a body well off the origin, is the OWNER's
   pass.
6. **Several primitive types.** The domain suite covers all six; the device
   journey covers a box. A cylinder, a cone, a capsule and a plane through the
   real controls.
7. **A body standing on the mirror plane, and one crossing it.** Both are
   covered arithmetically. Both produce a reflection coincident with or
   interpenetrating the original, which is a correct result and may still be a
   confusing picture. Is it?
8. **Hidden and locked source.** A hidden source produces a hidden reflection
   and a locked source a locked one — Stage 018A's Duplicate policy, reused
   rather than re-decided. Mirroring a hidden object therefore appears to do
   nothing in the viewport, and only the new row says otherwise. Is that the
   right answer, or should the reflection arrive visible?
9. **Naming.** `Bracket Mirror`, then `Bracket Mirror 2`. Is `Mirror` the right
   suffix beside `copy`, and should it be capitalised as it is?
10. **Undo / Redo feel.** One Mirror is one Undo, and Redo restores the same
    object. Does taking a reflection back land where the user expects when the
    reflection was the selected object?
11. **Selection outline and gizmo after a mirror.** The reflection becomes
    active, so it takes the outline and — if visible and unlocked — the transform
    handles. Does the outline read correctly on a body that may be
    interpenetrating its original?
12. **Handedness and palette presentation.** The strip, the Mirror glyph and the
    three plane chips are ordinary members of the Objects panel, so left-handed
    anchoring and the five palettes carry them like every other control. Neither
    is verified visually here.
13. **The Mirror glyph.** Two triangles facing each other across a dashed plane
    line, at 24 dp. Is "reflect across a plane" readable without the content
    description? **The exact visual icon design is OWNER LATER and no approval
    is claimed.**
